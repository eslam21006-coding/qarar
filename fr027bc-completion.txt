TRIGGER CONDITION (and why it differs from the spec's original literal)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Implemented as:  archetype ∈ {appointment, webinar}
                 AND cplCeiling !== null            (stage rates entered)
                 AND marketCplBenchmark != null
                 AND marketCplBenchmark > cplCeiling  (both in INPUT currency)

The spec originally wrote the condition as `unitTarget > cplCeiling`. Tracing
deriveTargets showed that is UNREACHABLE on the settings surface: the priority
chain is cplMedian30 → funnel-math (cplCeiling) → benchmark → null, so
  - when funnel math is the active tier, unitTarget === cplCeiling (never >);
  - the only tier that can push the judging target above the ceiling is
    30-day lead-cost history (cplMedian30), and the settings preview computes
    targets with baselines = null (funnel.get does the same), so that tier
    never fires here.

The user chose to compare the entered market benchmark directly against the
ceiling instead. Rationale: this fires at settings-entry time, BEFORE any real
spend history exists — catching a structurally broken funnel before ad budget
is wasted, rather than waiting ~30 days for history to confirm the same
conclusion. FR-027b (spec.md) and contracts/settings-fields.md were both
amended to describe this exact comparison and timing.
